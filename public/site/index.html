OUR BEST SERVICES

Managing Logistics For World’s Multinational Companies

We offer fast, reliable, and accurate worldwide delivery directly to your doorstep, factory, and warehouses.

Air Freight

We work with all major airlines; we can arrange the movement of expedited shipments, as well as cross-trade traffic, at the most economical rates. These consignments can range from small parcels to heavy lift cargo on specially chartered flights. Here, too, we are equipped to carry these consignments on a door-to-door or airport-to-airport basis, tailored to suit the exact needs of our clients.



Sea Freight

We offer a comprehensive export service for all your shipping needs, from small cartons to multiple container loads and break-bulk consignments to any destination worldwide, in the most efficient manner. We also work with shippers and importers to determine the most efficient mode of transportation for all cargos from their origin point to the final destination, offering a variety of comprehensive international transportation services.

Customs Clearing

No matter which type of freight you select, customs clearance is available. Our international network of licensed customs agents allows us to process all clearance requirements. A well-managed documentation process handled by our competent and efficient staff focuses on personalized monitoring and smoother clearance of shipments within 24 hours for air freight and 72 hours for ocean freight.


Export

We offer hassle-free, reliable, and cheap means to export your goods or personal items from Nigeria to any destination worldwide. We also specialize in large item packing, specialty crating, and heavy equipment shipping services.

Frequently Asked Questions

SkyFots Global Logistics operates as a delivery aggregator. We partner with a range of local and international courier companies. Once you make a request, we send the relevant information directly to your preferred delivery partner, who will perform the delivery within the agreed-upon schedule.

The cost of delivery is determined by the courier service selected for each specific delivery. Certain services may base their charges on the weight of the packages, while some others will charge according to the distance between pickup and delivery locations. You can use our free online delivery calculator to estimate the cost of your deliveries.

We provide delivery services to more than 185 countries worldwide.

In order to process a delivery, you need to create an account. Once your account is created, you can select the "Ship a package in minutes" option from the dashboard and follow the instructions to provide a pickup address, delivery address, and further details regarding the items in your package. After making the payment, your package will be processed.

You don't have to do anything to get lower delivery rates with us. Our delivery rates are automatically cheaper across the board. Sign up for free and start shipping to enjoy these lower rates!

Since we have a wide range of courier service providers, you can ship almost any item. When choosing a courier service for a delivery, you'll want to ensure that they are able to deliver the specific product you intend to ship. For example, All dry food items must be vacuum sealed before they can be shipped, as required by DHL Express.

With SkyFots Global Logistics, you always stay in control of your deliveries. You're always guaranteed a refund if your delivery is not completed. Once a delivery is arranged, you have the option to cancel until the package is picked up.
